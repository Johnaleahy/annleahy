Meet Ann Leahy, ICF-Certified Coach

We all have goals we want to achieve, challenges we strive to overcome, ideas that need to be developed, and times when we feel stuck. Working with me will create deep awareness of your potential and guide you on a dedicated path toward personal and professional well-being.

I bring an extensive background in leading teams, coaching, education, and social work to every partnership. My practice is centered on building the trust, safety, and deep connection required to allow people to think, learn, and change together.

I will meet you exactly where you are, working side by side with you to spark growth, foster sustainable change, and accompany you on your journey toward a more fulfilling life.
